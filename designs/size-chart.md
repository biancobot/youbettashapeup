# Size chart: millimetres at each carat weight

For each shape, the dimensions (length x width, or one figure for square or round outlines) that the
app's weight estimator gives for the template's default proportions, beside Stuller's trade
conversion chart (approximate, reflecting Stuller's loose-diamond inventory). The estimator is the
trade formula carats = length x width x depth x factor; a template with other proportions gives
other sizes, and the app always sizes the pavé background from the loaded design.
Generated by `node tools/sizes.mjs`.
