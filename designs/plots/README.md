# Lab plotting diagrams used as underlays

Crown and pavilion halves of the plotting diagram printed on publicly posted IGI diamond
reports, cropped from the report PDFs. They are reference images for tracing a design in the
app's underlay; they are the laboratories' artwork and are kept here only for that purpose.

| marquise-french-gia.png | GIA 4Cs, "How to Choose a Marquise Diamond" wireframe (French tips), crown view, callout removed |

Report PDFs are at `https://pdf.igi.org/<report id>.pdf`. The GIA wireframe is at
`https://4cs.gia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/CON-225-960x800-wireframes.jpg`.
