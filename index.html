Gem Facet Designer build 37

GemCad-style facet design: every facet is a plane by angle, index and distance; the stone is their intersection. Drag to rotate, shift-drag to roll, scroll to zoom, click a vertex to pick a meet point.

Design

Proportions rebuild the whole design: the facets follow the new outline, angles and heights by the same meet-point rules.
Girdle preform (outline)
A preform is a tier of 90° girdle facets whose distances follow the outline, as in GemCad. Design a fancy cut by adding tiers over it and cutting to meet points.
fold, mirror
NameAngleDistanceIndices
Angle: 0 = table, 90 = girdle facet, negative = pavilion. Distance: centre to facet. Click a row to select it; click near a vertex in the view to select a meet point, then meet cuts the tier through it.
Notes (ideal dimensions, sources)

Plot underlay

The plot's dark lines are drawn in red over the stone, scaled so its girdle width matches the stone's width. Use the front view for the crown plot and the back view for the pavilion plot.

Drawing sheet

To add a projected view, click L, R, A or B beside a view in the list below: it projects a new view to the Left, Right, Above or Below that view under the selected standard. Projected views can be projected from again, and all of them follow the base view as you rotate it. Gaps are in stone widths between view bounding boxes. Sheets are always orthographic.
