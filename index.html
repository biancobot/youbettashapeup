Gem Facet Designer

GemCad-style facet design: every facet is a plane by angle, index and distance; the stone is their intersection. Drag to rotate, shift-drag to roll, scroll to zoom, click a vertex to pick a meet point.

Girdle preform (outline)
A preform is a tier of 90° girdle facets whose distances follow the outline, as in GemCad. Design a fancy cut by adding tiers over it and cutting to meet points.
